## 1. Framing: a leading framework is not a particle discovery

Inflation is the working language of early-universe cosmology and, at the same time, not a completed empirical identification of a field. Our umbrella claim is Frontier: a quasi-exponential inflationary phase preceded the hot big bang and seeded cosmic structure (`cosmic_inflation_early_universe`; Guth, 1981; Planck Collaboration, 2020b). That light means: the framework solves classic puzzles, fits precision CMB spectra under slow-roll assumptions, and remains without a unique microphysical inflaton or a detected primordial tensor mode (Guth, 1981; Planck Collaboration, 2020b).

We separate layers that popular accounts often fuse:

| Layer | Map status | Content |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Hot big bang + CMB | 🟢 Established | Relic radiation, recombination (`cmb_hot_big_bang`) |
| Expansion / acceleration | 🟢 / 🔵 | Metric expansion; late-time acceleration |
| Inflation as framework | 🟠 Frontier | Horizon/flatness + near-scale-invariant seeds |
| Slow-roll preference | 🟠 Frontier | Planck constraints on \(n_s\), \(r\) |
| Primordial B-modes | 🟠 Frontier | Undetected; upper limits on \(r\) |
| Inflation vs bounce | 🟡 Competing | Working standard vs non-inflationary alternatives |
| Eternal inflation / multiverse | 🔴 Speculative | Theoretical extrapolation |
| Bounce *replaces* hot big bang | 🔴 Speculative | Minority replacement programme |

No confidence percentages. The Discussion follows the cluster downward from framework to tensors, alternatives, and the red ceiling.

## 2. Why inflation became the default

Guth’s inflationary universe proposed a brief accelerated expansion that could solve the horizon and flatness problems and generate density perturbations from quantum fluctuations of a scalar field (Guth, 1981). In the decades since, the combination of a nearly scale-invariant scalar spectrum and acoustic-peak CMB physics has made inflation the default early-universe story against which alternatives are compared (Planck Collaboration, 2020b).

Default is not Established in our taxonomy. Established is reserved for claims with multiple independent direct lines and textbook closure without mainstream rivals—as with cosmic expansion and the CMB as hot-big-bang relic radiation (Hubble, 1929; Penzias & Wilson, 1965; Planck Collaboration, 2020a). Inflation’s microphysics remains open: many potentials fit current data; the energy scale is not measured; the initial conditions of inflation are themselves debated (Guth, 1981; Planck Collaboration, 2020b).

## 3. What Planck actually prefers

Planck constraints on primordial spectra report \(n_s < 1\) and tight limits on running and tensors, favouring simple slow-roll, often plateau-like, models over large regions of older large-field parameter space (`inflation_slow_roll_planck`; Planck Collaboration, 2020b; Guth, 1981). That is a **class preference**, not an identification of the inflaton. The claim is Frontier: the data bite hard on model space, yet leave a family of viable scenarios (Planck Collaboration, 2020b).

Epistemic caution: “Planck confirms inflation” is true only in the weak sense that inflationary slow-roll templates fit. It is false if heard as “Planck discovered the inflaton field.” Our map records the former and refuses the latter.

## 4. Tensors: the missing half of the classic test

A classic inflationary prediction is a spectrum of primordial gravitational waves, partially characterised by the tensor-to-scalar ratio \(r\). Degree-scale B-mode polarisation experiments, notably BICEP/Keck after careful Galactic dust modelling, report no significant primordial B-mode excess and set leading upper limits on \(r\) (`primordial_tensors_undetected`; BICEP/Keck Collaboration, 2022; Planck Collaboration, 2020b). Joint Planck and BICEP/Keck analyses further trim inflationary model space (BICEP/Keck Collaboration, 2022; Planck Collaboration, 2020b).

Null results are knowledge. They do not kill inflation; they kill or pressure high-\(r\) corners. Future CMB-S4 / LiteBIRD-class experiments can move this light by detecting \(r > 0\) or by driving limits so low that broad classes of high-scale models become untenable (BICEP/Keck Collaboration, 2022). Until a positive primordial detection exists, tensors remain an upper-limit Frontier, not a discovery claim.

## 5. The yellow node: inflation versus non-inflationary alternatives

We record a Competing claim between inflationary hot-big-bang cosmology and non-inflationary early-universe alternatives such as bounces (`inflation_vs_noninflation_alts`; Planck Collaboration, 2020b; Ijjas & Steinhardt, 2018). This is not false balance. Inflation is the working standard with detailed CMB success under slow-roll (Planck Collaboration, 2020b). Bounce-class programmes remain actively published attempts to avoid singular beginnings or to replace quasi–de Sitter inflation while still generating perturbations (Ijjas & Steinhardt, 2018).

Decisive evidence has not eliminated every alternative. Nor have alternatives displaced inflation in mainstream data analysis. The yellow light means: both camps appear in the peer-reviewed literature; neither has a community-accepted knockout observable that the other side concedes. Candidate discriminants include specific non-Gaussianity shapes, primordial features, and tensor properties—if and when measured (Planck Collaboration, 2020b; Ijjas & Steinhardt, 2018).

A related but stronger Speculative claim asserts that a cyclic or bouncing cosmology *replaces* the hot big bang as the correct description (`cyclic_or_bounce_replaces_bb`; Ijjas & Steinhardt, 2018). That is a replacement thesis, not merely an alternative early phase, and is not mainstream consensus. Keep the Competing node (framework rivalry) separate from the Speculative replacement ceiling.

## 6. The red ceiling: eternal inflation and the multiverse

Some inflationary dynamics generically lead to eternal inflation and a multiverse of pocket universes (`eternal_inflation_multiverse`; Guth, 1981). CMB data test potentials and spectra; they do not observationally establish a multiverse ontology (Planck Collaboration, 2020b). The claim is Speculative: theoretically motivated in parts of the landscape, philosophically loaded, and not accepted as empirical consensus.

This ceiling matters for public communication. Sliding from “inflation fits \(n_s\)” to “we live in a multiverse” is a light jump our constitution forbids without new evidence. Theoretical interest is allowed; Established status is not.

## 7. What would move the lights

| Empirical development | Map consequence (illustrative) |
|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Robust primordial B-mode detection at \(r > 0\) | Move tensors from limits to a positive Frontier/Strong debate; constrain scale |
| \(r\) limits exclude broad high-scale classes | Shrink viable inflation model space; pressure some multiverse rhetorics |
| Observable that community agrees bounce fits and inflation fails (or vice versa) | Resolve or reshape `inflation_vs_noninflation_alts` |
| Direct or unique microphysical inflaton identification | Could eventually promote framework claims—extraordinarily high bar |
| No new data, only ontology essays | Must not move lights |

## 8. Synthesis

Inflation is best described as a **Frontier standard**: indispensable for much of precision early-universe analysis, underdetermined as microphysics, and incomplete as a tensor detection (Guth, 1981; Planck Collaboration, 2020b; BICEP/Keck Collaboration, 2022). Slow-roll preferences and \(r\) limits are real constraints, not vibes (Planck Collaboration, 2020b; BICEP/Keck Collaboration, 2022). Alternatives deserve a Competing node without being promoted to default (Ijjas & Steinhardt, 2018). Eternal inflation and multiverse readings remain Speculative extrapolations (Guth, 1981; Planck Collaboration, 2020b).

Cross-link to the H0 cluster: early dark energy and other pre-recombination extensions aimed at the Hubble tension (`early_dark_energy_H0_fix`; Poulin et al., 2019) are **not** the same claim as inflation. They may sit in the same early-universe toolbox; they must not be merged into “inflation solves H0.” Cross-link to bedrock: none of the inflation debate undoes the Established CMB hot-big-bang relic (`cmb_hot_big_bang`; Penzias & Wilson, 1965; Planck Collaboration, 2020a).
